The **Options** button (General panel) sets two things.

## Panels shown in the ribbon

Tick the panels to show. The General panel (About, Options) is always visible. The change applies immediately.

## Window appearance

Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Set by Revit** for the tools' windows. The choice applies the next time a window opens.

<Aside>
Ribbon icons always follow the Revit theme. “Set by Revit” is only available with Revit 2024 and later; before that, windows are light.
</Aside>

Click **Save** to confirm. Settings are specific to this computer and this Revit version.
